بودنغ الزبيب الإنجليزي

طبق بريطاني تقليدي من Spotted Dick بالنكهات الكلاسيكية والأسلوب الإنجليزي

⏱ 38 دق 👥 4 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.7 (1066 تقييم) 🔥 557 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 250g Self-raising Flour
  • pinch Salt
  • 125g Suet
  • 175g Currants
  • 80g Caster Sugar
  • Zest of 1 Lemon
  • Zest of 1 Orange
  • 150ml Milk
  • to serve Custard

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Put the flour and salt in a bowl. Add the suet, currants, sugar, lemon and orange zest.
  2. Pour in 150ml milk and mix to a firm but moist dough, adding the extra milk if necessary.
  3. Shape into a fat roll about 20cm long. Place on a large rectangle of baking parchment. Wrap loosely to allow for the pud
  4. Place a steamer over a large pan of boiling water, add the pudding to the steamer, cover and steam for 1 1/2 hours. Top
  5. Remove from the steamer and allow to cool slightly before unwrapping. Serve sliced with custard.
