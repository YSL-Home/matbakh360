🧺 المكونات

  • 1 lb Shrimp
  • 2 chopped Scotch Bonnet
  • 1 tablespoon All-purpose Seasoning

  • 2 tsp Allspice
  • 3/4 cup Shrimp Stock
  • 1/4 cup Onion
  • 2 cloves minced Garlic
  • 2 tablespoons White Vinegar
  • 3 sprigs Thyme
  • To taste Salt
