فيستيفال الجامايكي

اكتشف وصفة Jamaican Festival (Sweet Dumpling) التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 53 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.3 (957 تقييم) 🔥 318 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 1 1/2 cups All purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup Cornmeal
  • 6 tablespoons Sugar
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 3/4 cup Milk
  • For frying Oil

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Heat a heavy bottom pot of oil that has at least 3 inches of oil in it or use a deep fryer if you have one. Turn the hea
  2. Add the all-purpose flour, cornmeal, granulated sugar, baking powder, and salt to a large bowl and stir to combine.
  3. Add the vanilla extract and milk and stir until the dough comes together. Then use your hands to lightly form the mixtur
  4. Pinch off pieces of dough and roll them into long oval shapes. Make about 12 dumplings.
  5. Once the oil has reached the temperature of 350 degrees Fahrenheit, fry the dough on all sides, until golden brown. This
  6. Remove dough and drain off any excess grease. Serve and enjoy.
