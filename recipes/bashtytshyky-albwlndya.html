👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Sift flour and salt into a large mixing bowl.
  2. Use a spoon to push the egg yolk through a fine sieve into the flour.
  3. Add the raw egg and mix well.
  4. Beat in butter 1 tablespoon at a time.
