سمك إسكوفيتش الجامايكي

اكتشف وصفة Escovitch Fish التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 43 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.4 (1332 تقييم) 🔥 508 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 2 Pounds Red Snapper
  • 1/2 cup Vegetable Oil
  • 1 clove peeled crushed Garlic
  • 1/2 tsp Ginger
  • 2 sprigs Thyme
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 0.5 Red Pepper
  • 0.5 Yellow Pepper
  • 1 sliced Onion
  • 1 chopped Carrots
  • 1 tbs Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Allspice

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Rinse fish; rub with lemon or lime, seasoned with salt and pepper or use your favorite seasoning. I used creole seasonin
  2. In large skillet heat oil over medium heat, until hot, add the fish, cook each side- for about 5-7 minutes until cooked
  3. Add, bay leave, garlic and ginger, stir-fry for about a minute making sure the garlic does not burn
  4. Then add onion, bell peppers, thyme, scotch bonnet, sugar, all spice-continue stirring for about 2-3 minutes. Add vinega
  5. Discard bay leave, thyme spring and serve over fish with a side of this bammy. You may make the sauce about 2 days in ad
