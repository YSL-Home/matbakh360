يخنة السمك البرتغالية

اكتشف وصفة Portuguese fish stew (Caldeirada de peixe) التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 29 دق 👥 2 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.6 (1825 تقييم) 🔥 236 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 2 finely chopped Onions
  • 1 Diced Red Pepper
  • Small bunch Coriander
  • 1 small Red Chilli
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 400ml Dry White Wine
  • Pinch Saffron
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 300g Potatoes
  • 400g Plum Tomatoes
  • 600g Cod
  • 300g Squid

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large, deep-sided frying pan, and fry the onion and pepper on a medium heat until softened bu
  2. Add the potatoes, tomatoes, and 300ml water and bring to a gentle boil. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon on the side o
  3. Season well, then gently push the fish into the sauce, and arrange the squid, prawns, clams and mussels on the surface.
