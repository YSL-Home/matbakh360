كمبير التركي

وصفة تركية شهية من Kumpir بالبهارات العثمانية والنكهات الشرقية

⏱ 33 دق 👥 4 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.5 (1080 تقييم) 🔥 398 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 2 large Potatoes
  • 2 tbs Butter
  • 150g Cheese
  • 1 large Onion
  • 1 large Red Pepper
  • Pinch Red Chilli Flakes

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. If you order kumpir in Turkey, the standard filling is first, lots of butter mashed into the potato, followed by cheese.
  2. Grate (roughly – you can use as much as you like) 150g of cheese.
  3. Finely chop one onion and one sweet red pepper.
  4. Put these ingredients into a large bowl with a good sprinkling of salt and pepper, chilli flakes (optional).
