سيري موكا ماليزية

اكتشف وصفة Seri muka kuih التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 39 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.1 (1262 تقييم) 🔥 533 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 400g Rice
  • 150ml Coconut Milk
  • 100ml Water
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 5 tbs Corn Flour
  • 3 tbs Flour
  • 3 Eggs
  • 200ml Coconut Cream
  • 140g Sugar

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Soak glutinous rice with water for at least 1 ½ hours prior to using. Drain.
  2. Prepare a 9-inch round or square cake pan and spray with cooking spray or line with plastic wrap.
  3. Mix coconut milk, water, salt and the rice. Pour it into cake pan, topped with the pandan knots.
  4. Steam for 30 minutes.
  5. After 30 minutes, fluff up the rice and remove pandan knots. Then, using a greased spatula, flatten the steamed rice. Ma
  6. Steam for another 10 minutes.
