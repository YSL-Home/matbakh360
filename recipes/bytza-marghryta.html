بيتزا مارغريتا

بيتزا إيطالية كلاسيكية بالطماطم والموزاريلا — وصفة إيطالي إيطالية

⏱ 44 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.8 (667 تقييم) 🔥 441 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 150ml Water
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 15g Yeast
  • 225g Plain Flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp Salt
  • Drizzle Olive Oil
  • 80g Passata
  • 70g Mozzarella
  • Peeled and Sliced Oregano
  • Leaves Basil
  • Pinch Black Pepper

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. 1 Preheat the oven to 230°C.
  2. 2 Add the sugar and crumble the fresh yeast into warm water.
  3. 3 Allow the mixture to stand for 10 – 15 minutes in a warm place (we find a windowsill on a sunny day works best) until
  4. 4 Sift the flour and salt into a large mixing bowl, make a well in the middle and pour in the yeast mixture and olive oi
  5. 5 Lightly flour your hands, and slowly mix the ingredients together until they bind.
  6. 6 Generously dust your surface with flour.
