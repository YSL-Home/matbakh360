فطيرة اللحم المفروم

طبق بريطاني تقليدي من Minced Beef Pie بالنكهات الكلاسيكية والأسلوب الإنجليزي

⏱ 35 دق 👥 4 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.1 (742 تقييم) 🔥 210 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 2 tbs Vegetable Oil
  • 500g Minced Beef
  • 1 chopped Onion
  • 1 tbls Tomato Puree
  • 1 ½ tbsp Plain Flour
  • 75g Mushrooms
  • 250ml Beef Stock
  • Dash Worcestershire Sauce
  • 400g Shortcrust Pastry
  • 1 Egg Yolks

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Preheat the oven to 200C/400F/Gas 6.
  2. Heat the oil in a deep frying pan and fry the beef mince for 4-5 minutes, breaking it up with a wooden spoon as it brown
  3. Add the onion and cook for 2-3 minutes, then stir in the tomato purée and cook for 2-3 more minutes. Stir in the flour a
  4. Roll out the pastry on a floured work surface until it is slightly larger than the pie dish. Gently drape the pastry ove
  5. Cut some leaf shapes out of the pastry trimmings and decorate the top of the pie, sticking them to the pastry with the b
  6. Make three or four slits in the pastry to allow the steam to escape, then brush the pie with the rest of the beaten egg
