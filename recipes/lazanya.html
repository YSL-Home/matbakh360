لازانيا

وصفة إيطالية كلاسيكية لطبق Lasagne بأجود المكونات والنكهات الأصيلة

⏱ 53 دق 👥 4 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.2 (1382 تقييم) 🔥 513 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 1 tblsp Olive Oil
  • 2 Bacon
  • 1 finely chopped Onion
  • 1 Stick Celery
  • 1 medium Carrots
  • 2 cloves chopped Garlic
  • 500g Minced Beef
  • 1 tbls Tomato Puree
  • 800g Chopped Tomatoes
  • 1 tblsp Honey
  • 500g Lasagne Sheets
  • 400ml Creme Fraiche

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan. Use kitchen scissors to snip the bacon into small pieces, or use a sharp knife to chop
  2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 min, then tip in the mince and cook, stirring and breaking it up with a wooden spoon, for
  3. Stir in the tomato purée and cook for 1 min, mixing in well with the beef and vegetables. Tip in the chopped tomatoes. F
  4. Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. To assemble the lasagne, ladle a little of the ragu sauce into the bottom of the roast
  5. Put the crème fraîche in a bowl and mix with 2 tbsp water to loosen it and make a smooth pourable sauce. Pour this over
