عصيدة الذرة الجامايكية

اكتشف وصفة Jamaican Cornmeal Porridge التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 50 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.6 (827 تقييم) 🔥 433 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 4 cups Water
  • 380g Coconut Milk
  • 1 cup Fine Yellow Cornmeal
  • 1 1/2 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp Ground Allspice
  • 1/2 cup Sweetened Condensed Milk

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Add 2 ½ cups water, 1 can coconut milk to a 4 QT heavy bottomed pot. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. Meanwhile, add 1 cup cornmeal and 1 ½ cup water to a large mixing cup and whisk until smooth.

  4. Cook over low heat for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally. When there is about 5 minutes left, stir in vanilla extract
  5. Serve and enjoy.
