فطير مشلتت

فطير مصري ورقي بالسمن البلدي — وصفة سريع مصرية

⏱ 29 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.7 (995 تقييم) 🔥 564 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 4 cups Flour
  • 1 1/2 cups Water
  • 1/4 tsp Salt
  • 1 cup Unsalted Butter
  • 1/4 cup Olive Oil

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Mix the flour and salt then pour one cup of water and start kneading.
  2. If you feel the dough is still not coming together or too dry, gradually add the remaining water until you get a dough t
  3. Let the dough rest for just 10 minutes then divide the dough into 6-8 balls depending on the size you want for your fete
  4. Warm up the butter/ghee or oil you are using and pour into a deep bowl.
  5. Immerse the dough balls into the warm butter. Let it rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. Preheat oven to 550F.
