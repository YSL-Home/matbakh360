فطائر الفواكه المجففة

طبق بريطاني تقليدي من Mince Pies بالنكهات الكلاسيكية والأسلوب الإنجليزي

⏱ 58 دق 👥 2 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.9 (199 تقييم) 🔥 252 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 225g Butter
  • 350g Plain Flour
  • 100g Caster Sugar
  • 280g Mincemeat
  • 1 small Egg
  • Topping Icing Sugar

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. To make the pastry, rub 225g cold, diced butter into 350g plain flour, then mix in 100g golden caster sugar and a pinch
  2. Combine the pastry into a ball – don’t add liquid – and knead it briefly. The dough will be fairly firm, like shortbread
  3. Preheat the oven to 200C/gas 6/fan 180C. Line 18 holes of two 12-hole patty tins, by pressing small walnut-sized balls o
  4. Spoon 280g mincemeat into the pies.
  5. Take slightly smaller balls of pastry than before and pat them out between your hands to make round lids, big enough to
  6. Top the pies with their lids, pressing the edges gently together to seal – you don’t need to seal them with milk or egg
