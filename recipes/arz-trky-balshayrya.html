أرز تركي بالشعيرية

وصفة تركية شهية من Turkish rice (vermicelli rice) بالبهارات العثمانية والنكهات الشرقية

⏱ 35 دق 👥 5 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.9 (1291 تقييم) 🔥 520 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 300g Rice
  • 3 tablespoons Vegetable Oil
  • 70g Vermicelli Pasta
  • 30g Unsalted Butter
  • 600ml Chicken Stock

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Pour the rice into a very large bowl under cold running water and carefully drain the water out of the bowl through a si
  2. Put the oil into a medium pan over medium heat. Add the vermicelli and stir continuously until the strands turn a rich g
  3. Drain the rice thoroughly through a sieve. Add the rice to the pan and stir well. Pour the hot stock into the pan, sprin
