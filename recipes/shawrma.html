شاورما

🧺 المكونات

  • 1 kg Chicken Thighs
  • 1 tbs Coriander
  • 1 tbs Cumin
  • 1 tbs Cardamom
  • 1 tsp Cayenne Pepper
  • 2 tsp Paprika
  • 2 tbs Lemon Juice
  • 3 tbs Olive Oil
  • 1 cup Greek Yogurt
  • 1 Garlic Clove
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • Splash Lemon Juice
