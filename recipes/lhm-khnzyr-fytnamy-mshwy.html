لحم خنزير فيتنامي مشوي

اكتشف وصفة Vietnamese Grilled Pork (bun-thit-nuong) التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 54 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.8 (1017 تقييم) 🔥 370 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 1.5 pounds sliced Pork
  • 1 package thin Rice Vermicelli
  • 4-6 Egg Rolls
  • 3 tablespoons minced Challots
  • 1.5 tablespoons minced garlic Garlic
  • ¼ cup Sugar
  • 1 tablespoon Fish Sauce
  • ½ tablespoon thick Soy sauce
  • ½ tablespoon Pepper
  • 3 tablespoons Olive Oil
  • Sliced Cucumber
  • Leaves Mint

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Slice the uncooked pork thinly, about ⅛". It helps to slightly freeze it (optional).
  2. Mince garlic and shallots. Mix in a bowl with sugar, fish sauce, thick soy sauce, pepper, and oil until sugar dissolves.
  3. Marinate the meat for 1 hour, or overnight for better results.
  4. Bake the pork at 375F for 10-15 minutes or until about 80% cooked. Finish cooking by broiling in the oven until a nice g
  5. Assemble your bowl with veggies, noodles, and garnish. Many like to mix the whole bowl up and pour the fish sauce on top
