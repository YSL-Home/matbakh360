ضأل مشوي بالقرفة والشمر

🧺 المكونات

  • 1 Lamb Leg
  • Zest and juice of 1 Lemon
  • 4 tablespoons Olive Oil
  • 2 tablespoons Clear Honey
  • 1 tablespoon Cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon Fennel Seeds
  • 1 tablespoon Ground Cumin
  • 3 Cloves Crushed Garlic
