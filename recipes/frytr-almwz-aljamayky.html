فريتر الموز الجامايكي

اكتشف وصفة Jamaican Banana Fritters التقليدية بمكوناتها الأصيلة وطريقة تحضيرها الشهية

⏱ 44 دق 👥 5 أشخاص 📊 سهل ⭐ 4.3 (1586 تقييم) 🔥 213 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 3 Medium Banana
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1/2 tsp Cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp Nutmeg
  • 3 tablespoons Brown Sugar
  • Pinch Salt
  • 1 cup All purpose flour
  • For frying Oil

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. Mash bananas with fork in bowl.
  2. Add brown sugar, vanilla, extract, cinnamon, nutmeg and a pinch of salt. Sift in flour and stir with spoon until fully c
  3. Add oil to a 10 inch skillet over medium-high heat. Once oil is hot, drop banana fritter batter in oil by the spoonful.
  4. Remove fritters and drain on paper towels. Enjoy
