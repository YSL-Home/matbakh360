Singapore Noodles with Shrimp

وصفة Singapore Noodles with Shrimp التقليدية — وصفة بحري صينية

⏱ 40 دق 👥 3 أشخاص 📊 متوسط ⭐ 4.2 (1859 تقييم) 🔥 378 سعرة

🧺 المكونات

  • 2 tsp Sesame Seed Oil
  • 2 tablespoons Soy Sauce
  • 2 tablespoons Seasoned Rice Vinegar
  • 6 oz Vermicelli Rice Noodles
  • 2 large Egg
  • 2 tblsp Ground Ginger
  • 1 clove finely chopped Garlic
  • 2 Carrots
  • 1 sliced Jalapeno
  • 1/2 Onion

👨‍🍳 طريقة التحضير

  1. For the sweet onion, look for Vidalia, OSO Sweet, or Walla Walla. The super-sweet varieties are more suited to this stir-fry because at the end of cooking, the onion still has a slight crunch.
  2. In a bowl, combine the sesame oil, soy sauce, and rice vinegar.
  3. Cook the rice noodles:
  4. Bring a large saucepan of water to a boil, add the noodles, and use tongs to turn them so they are submerged. Cook for 2 minutes, or until they are tender but still have some bite (they will cook a little more once you add them to the skillet).
  5. Drain, rinse with cold water, and use scissors to snip the noodles several times to break them up into shorter lengths.
  6. Scramble the eggs:
  7. In a small bowl whisk together the eggs. Heat the skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add 1 tablespoon of the peanut or canola oil. Add the eggs and scramble them for 2 minutes, or until they form large, soft curds. Transfer them from the pan to a plate or bowl.
  8. Cook the vegetables:
  9. Add 1 tablespoon of the remaining oil to the pan. When it is hot, add the ginger, garlic, carrots, jalapeño, onion, and salt. Cook, stirring constantly, for 2 minutes or until the vegetables start to soften.
  10. Add the remaining ingredients:
  11. Sprinkle the vegetable mixture with the remaining 1 tablespoon peanut or canola oil. When the oil is hot, add the ham, cabbage, scallions, red pepper, and curry powder to the pan. Cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.
  12. Add the shrimp and cook, stirring, for 3 more minutes or until the shrimp are bright pink and cooked through.
