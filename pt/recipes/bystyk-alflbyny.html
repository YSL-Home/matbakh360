Ingredients

  • 1 lb Beef
  • 5 tablespoons Soy Sauce
  • 1 Lemon
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 3 parts Onion
  • 4 tbs Olive Oil
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 pinch Salt

Instructions

  1. 0. Marinate beef in soy sauce, lemon (or calamansi), and ground black pepper for at least 1 hour. Note: marinate overnig
  2. 1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan then pan-fry half of the onions until the texture becomes soft. Set aside
  3. 2. Drain the marinade from the beef. Set it aside. Pan-fry the beef on the same pan where the onions were fried for 1 mi
  4. 3. Add more oil if needed. Saute garlic and remaining raw onions until onion softens.
  5. 4. Pour the remaining marinade and water. Bring to a boil.
  6. 5. Add beef. Cover the pan and simmer until the meat is tender. Note: Add water as needed.
