Ingredients

  • 300g Squid
  • 200g Plain Flour
  • 2 tblsp Capers
  • 1 clove peeled crushed Garlic
  • 5 tblsp Mayonnaise
  • For frying Vegetable Oil
  • To serve Lemon

Instructions

  1. Cut the squid into rings about ½cm thick. Tip the flour into a freezer bag and season well. Add the capers, then give ev
  2. Pour some oil into a large pan until it comes about 7cm up the sides, but the pan is no more than a third full. Place ov
  3. Remove a handful of squid from the flour and shake off any excess. Gently drop into the oil, then cook for about 3 mins
