  • 450ml Chicken Stock
  • 3 Chicken Breast

  • 2 sliced Leek
  • 2 cloves minced Garlic
  • 50g Plain Flour
  • 200ml Milk
  • 3 tbs White Wine
  • 150ml Double Cream
  • 150g Ham
  • spinkling Sea Salt
  • pinch Pepper
