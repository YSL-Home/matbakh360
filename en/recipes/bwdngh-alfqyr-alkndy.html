Pouding chomeur

Pouding chomeur — authentic Canadian Dessert recipe with step-by-step instructions, ingredients and photos.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup Butter
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 2 cups Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 1/4 cup Milk
  • 1 1/2 cup Maple Syrup
  • 1 1/2 cup Brown Sugar
  • 1 1/2 cup Single Cream
  • 1/3 cup Butter

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, with an electric mixer, mix the butter and sugar till the mix is light.
  2. Add eggs and vanilla and mix.
  3. In another bowl, mix flour and baking powder.
  4. Alternate flour mix and milk to the butter mix.
  5. Pour into a 13 inch by 9 inch greased pan.
  6. In a large casserole, bring to boil the syrup, brown sugar, cream and butter and constantly stir.
  7. Reduce heat and and gently cook 2 minutes or till sauce has reduced a little bit.
  8. Pour sauce gently over cake.
  9. Bake at 325°f (160°c) about 35 minutes or till cake is light brown and when toothpick inserted comes out clean.
