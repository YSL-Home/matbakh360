Ajo blanco

Ajo blanco — authentic Spanish Starter recipe with step-by-step instructions, ingredients and photos.

Ingredients

  • 150g White bread
  • 200g Almonds
  • 50 ml Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • 1 Garlic Clove
  • 1 ½ tbsp Red Wine Vinegar

Instructions

  1. Tip the bread into a bowl and pour over 350ml water. Leave to soak for 10 mins.
  2. Blend the ingredients together with 350ml water and 1 tsp salt.
  3. Let the soup cool in the fridge for 1 hr or so, then serve with a drizzle of oil and some black pepper.
