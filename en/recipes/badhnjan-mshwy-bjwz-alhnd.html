Grilled eggplant with coconut milk

Grilled eggplant with coconut milk — authentic Filipino Vegetarian recipe with step-by-step instructions, ingredients and photos.

Ingredients

  • 6 Egg Plants
  • 1 can Coconut Milk
  • 1 tbs Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • To taste Red Pepper Flakes
  • 4 Sticks Onions

Instructions

  1. . Prepare the eggplants for grilling by pricking them all over with a fork. This is so it won’t burst during the grilling process as the natural water in it heats up.
  2. Grill the eggplants, turning them over frequently to ensure even cooking. Grill until the skins are dark brown, even black and the eggplant is soft when you touch it.
  3. Soak the grilled eggplant in a bowl of water to cool it down. Peel the skin off the eggplant. Place the whole eggplants in a shallow dish (my mom actually cuts the eggplant into small, bite-sized pieces).
  4. 4. In a small mixing bowl, mix together the coconut milk or cream, lemon powder, salt and hot pepper. Mix until the lemon powder and salt dissolve. Taste, then adjust the amount of lemon powder, salt and hot pepper to your liking. Pour the mixture over the eggplant. Sprinkle the green onions over the eggplant and coconut milk. Stir gently to combine.
