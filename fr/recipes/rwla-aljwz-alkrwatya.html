Ingrédients

  • 450g Flour
  • 55g Caster Sugar
  • 2 parts Yeast
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 6 oz Milk
  • 2 Beaten Eggs
  • 30g Butter
  • 140g Walnuts
  • 85g Butter
  • 85g Brown Sugar
  • 1 tsp Cinnamon
  • To Glaze Milk
