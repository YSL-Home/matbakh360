Torrijas au xérès

Torrijas au sherry — authentique recette de petit-déjeuner espagnol avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 medium Egg
  • 2 tbsp Double Cream
  • 2 tblsp Milk
  • 3 tsp Golden Caster Sugar
  • 3 tablespoons Sweet Sherry
  • 4 thick slices Bread
  • For frying Olive Oil
  • To serve Icing Sugar
  • To serve Creme Fraiche

Préparation

  1. In a wide, shallow bowl, beat the eggs with the cream, milk, golden caster sugar and sherry. Cut each slice of bread in
  2. Heat 1½ tbsp olive oil in a large frying pan and cook the bread for about 3 mins on each side until dark golden and cris
  3. Divide the torrijas between plates and dust with the icing sugar. Serve with crème fraîche or Greek yogurt on the side.
