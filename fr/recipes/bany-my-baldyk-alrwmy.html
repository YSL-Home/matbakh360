Turquie Bánh mì

Turkey Bánh mì — recette diverse vietnamienne authentique avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 small Baguette
  • 50g Chicken Liver
  • 1/4 Cucumber
  • 140g Turkey
  • 1 tablespoon Mayonnaise
  • 1 sliced Red Chilli
  • Handful Mint
  • 2 small Carrots
  • 75g Red Cabbage
  • 1/4 tsp Ground Ginger
  • ½ tablespoon Rice Vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon Golden Caster Sugar

Préparation

  1. To make the pickled slaw, tip the carrots and cabbage into a large bowl. In a small bowl, combine the ginger, rice vineg
  2. Halve the baguettes lengthways and spread the pâté over the bottom half. Top with the pickled slaw, cucumber and turkey.
