Soupe crémeuse aux tomates

Soupe crémeuse aux tomates — authentique recette British Starter avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 chopped Onions
  • 2 sticks Celery
  • 300g Carrots
  • 500g Potatoes
  • 4 Bay Leaf
  • 5 tblsp Tomato Puree
  • 2 tblsp Sugar
  • 2 tblsp White Vinegar
  • 1½ kg Chopped Tomatoes
  • 500g Passata
  • 3 Vegetable Stock Cube

Préparation

  1. Put the oil, onions, celery, carrots, potatoes and bay leaves in a big casserole dish, or two saucepans. Fry gently unti
  2. Stir in the tomato purée, sugar, vinegar, chopped tomatoes and passata, then crumble in the stock cubes. Add 1 litre boi
  3. To serve, reheat the soup, stirring in the milk – try not to let it boil. Serve in small bowls with cheesy sausage rolls
