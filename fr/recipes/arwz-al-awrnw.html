Arroz al horno (riz cuit au four)

Arroz al horno (riz cuit au four) — recette authentique de porc espagnol avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • 800g Pork belly slices
  • 150g Black Pudding
  • 100g Bacon lardon
  • 1 chopped Onion
  • 2 Red Pepper
  • 1 chopped Plum Tomatoes
  • 8 Garlic Clove
  • 4 teaspoons Paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon Chilli Flakes
  • 200g Dried white beans
  • 1 1/2 L Chicken Stock

Préparation

  1. Heat oven to 200C/180C/gas 6. Heat half the oil in a deep frying or sauté pan (or shallow casserole dish) measuring arou
  2. Sprinkle the rice around the pork belly, pushing it underneath the stock. Let the stock come to the boil again, season w
  3. Squeeze lemon juice over the top and drizzle over some extra virgin olive oil just before serving, if you like.
