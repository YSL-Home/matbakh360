Linguine de crevettes au piment

Linguine de crevettes au piment — authentique recette de pâtes italiennes avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 280g Linguine Pasta
  • 200g Sugar Snap Peas
  • 2 tblsp Olive Oil
  • 2 cloves chopped Garlic Clove
  • 1 large Red Chilli
  • 24 Skinned King Prawns
  • 12 Cherry Tomatoes
  • Handful Basil Leaves
  • Leaves Lettuce
  • to serve Bread
  • 2 tbsp Fromage Frais
  • Grated Zest of 2 Lime

Préparation

  1. Mix the dressing ingredients in a small bowl and season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
  2. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions. Add the sugar snap peas for the last minute or so of cooking time.

  4. Add the tomatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, for 3 minutes until they just start to soften. Drain the pasta and sug
  5. Serve with salad leaves drizzled with the lime dressing, and warm crusty bread.
