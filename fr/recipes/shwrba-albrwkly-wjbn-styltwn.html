Soupe Brocoli & Stilton

Soupe Brocoli & Stilton — authentique recette British Starter avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 tblsp Rapeseed Oil
  • 1 finely chopped Onion
  • 1 Celery
  • 1 sliced Leek
  • 1 medium Potatoes
  • 1 knob Butter
  • 1 litre hot Vegetable Stock
  • 1 Head chopped Broccoli
  • 140g Stilton Cheese

Préparation

  1. Heat the rapeseed oil in a large saucepan and then add the onions. Cook on a medium heat until soft. Add a splash of wat
  2. Add the celery, leek, potato and a knob of butter. Stir until melted, then cover with a lid. Allow to sweat for 5 minute
  3. Pour in the stock and add any chunky bits of broccoli stalk. Cook for 10 – 15 minutes until all the vegetables are soft.
  4. Add the rest of the broccoli and cook for a further 5 minutes. Carefully transfer to a blender and blitz until smooth. S
