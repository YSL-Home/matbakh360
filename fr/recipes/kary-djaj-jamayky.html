Recette de poulet au curry jamaïcain

Recette de poulet au curry jamaïcain — recette authentique de poulet jamaïcain avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 Lbs Chicken Thighs
  • 2 1/2 Tbs Curry Powder
  • 1 tsp All-purpose Seasoning
  • 1 chopped Onion
  • 3 chopped Spring Onions
  • 1/2 Green Pepper
  • 4 Cloves Chopped Garlic
  • 1 chopped Scotch Bonnet
  • 3 sprigs Thyme
  • 3 tablespoons Olive Oil
  • 1/4 cup Water
  • 1 large Russet Potato

Préparation

  1. Instructions
  2. Add chicken thighs, all-purpose seasoning, 1 tablespoon of curry powder, onion, green onions, green pepper, garlic clove
  3. One the chicken is done marinating, remove onions, peppers, and garlic cloves from chicken and set aside.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large heavy bottom pot over medium heat. Add remaining curry powder and stir, cooking for about 20-3
  5. Add chicken and ¼ cup of water to the pot. Cover and reduce heat to medium low. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 30-35 m
  6. When there is about 15 minutes remaining, stir in potato and continue to cook covered.
