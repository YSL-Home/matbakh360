Ragu de canard vénitien

Venetian Duck Ragu — authentique recette de pâtes italiennes avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 1 tbls Olive Oil
  • 4 Duck Legs
  • 2 finely chopped Onions
  • 2 cloves minced Garlic
  • 2 tsp ground Cinnamon
  • 2 tsp Plain Flour
  • 250ml Red Wine
  • 800g Chopped Tomatoes
  • 1 Chicken Stock Cube
  • 3 sprigs Rosemary
  • 2 Bay Leaves
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Préparation

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan. Add the duck legs and brown on all sides for about 10 mins. Remove to a plate and set aside
  2. Carefully lift the duck legs out of the sauce and place on a plate – they will be very tender so try not to lose any of
  3. Cook the pasta following pack instructions, then drain, reserving a cup of the pasta water, and add the pasta to the rag
