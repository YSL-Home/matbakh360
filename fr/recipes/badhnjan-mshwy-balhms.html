Grillades à l'aubergine et au houmous

Grils à l'aubergine et au houmous — authentique recette végétarienne turque avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 2 Aubergine
  • 2 tablespoons Vegetable Oil
  • 3 sliced thinly Bread
  • 300g Hummus
  • 100g Walnuts
  • 40g Parsley
  • 200g Cherry Tomatoes
  • Juice of 1/2 Lemon
  • Splash Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Préparation

  1. Lay the aubergine out in one layer on a large baking sheet. Brush sparingly with vegetable oil, then season generously.
  2. Spread a couple of tsps of hummus on top of each slice of aubergine. Tip the breadcrumbs onto a large plate, then press
  3. Toss the walnuts, parsley and cherry tomatoes in a bowl, season, then add the lemon juice and olive oil and toss again.
