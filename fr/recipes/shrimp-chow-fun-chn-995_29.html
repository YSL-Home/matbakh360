Chow aux crevettes

Shrimp Chow Fun — authentique recette de fruits de mer chinois avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 1/2 bag Rice Stick Noodles
  • 8 oz Prawns
  • 1/2 Egg
  • pinch Pepper
  • 2 tsp Sesame Seed Oil
  • 2 tbs Cornstarch
  • 4 tbs Oil
  • 1 tsp Minced Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ginger
  • 1/2 cup Onion

Préparation

  1. - SOAK THE RICE NOODLES
  2. Soak the rice noodles overnight untill they are soft
  3. - BOIL THE RICE NOODLES
  4. Boil the noodles for 10-15 minutes and then rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process of the noodles.
  5. -MARINATING THE SHRIMP
  6. In a bowl add the shrimp, egg, 1 pinch of white pepper, 1 Teaspoon of sesame seed oil, 1 Tablespoon corn starch and 1 tablespoon of oil
  7. Mix together well
  8. In a wok add 2 Tablespoons of oil, shrimp and stir fry them until it is golden brown
  9. Set the shrimp aside
  10. Add 1 Tablespoon of oil to the work and then add minced garlic, ginger and all of the vegetables.
  11. Add the noodles to the wok
  12. Next add sherry cooking wine, oyster sauce, sugar, vinegar, sesame seed oil, 1 pinch white pepper, and soy sauce
