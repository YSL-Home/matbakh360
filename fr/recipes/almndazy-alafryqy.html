Mandazi fait maison

Mandazi fait maison — authentique recette de petit-déjeuner kenyan avec des instructions étape par étape, des ingrédients et des photos.

Ingrédients

  • 750g Self-raising Flour
  • 6 tablespoons Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup Milk

Préparation

  1. This is one recipe a lot of people have requested and I have tried to make it as simple as possible and I hope it will w
  2. You can use Coconut milk instead of regular milk, you can also add desiccated coconut to the dry flour or other spices l
  3. For “healthy looking” mandazis do not roll the dough too thin before frying and use the procedure I have indicated above
  4. 1. Mix the flour,cinnamon and sugar in a suitable bowl.
  5. 2. In a separate bowl whisk the egg into the milk
  6. 3. Make a well at the centre of the flour and add the milk and egg mixture and slowly mix to form a dough.
