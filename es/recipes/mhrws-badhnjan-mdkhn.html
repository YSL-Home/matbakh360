  • Juice of 1 Lemon
  • 3 Cloves Crushed Garlic
  • 150ml Yogurt

Instructions

  2. Using a sharp knife, score the grilled flesh and scoop out the flesh with a spoon. Tip into a bowl and mash with a fork
