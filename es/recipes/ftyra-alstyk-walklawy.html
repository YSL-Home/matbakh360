Ingredients

  • 300g Puff Pastry
  • Beaten Egg White
  • Beaten Egg Yolks
  • 2 tbs Vegetable Oil
  • 70 ml Beef
  • 200g Lamb Kidney
  • 2 chopped Onions
  • 30g Plain Flour
  • 85 ml Beef Stock
  • pinch Salt
  • pinch Pepper
  • Dash Worcestershire Sauce
