Ingredients

  • 4 pounded to 1cm thickness chicken breast
  • 2 tablespoons plain flour
  • 1 beaten egg
  • 100g fine breadcrumbs
  • 230ml frying vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons sunflower oil
  • 2 sliced onions
  • 5 chopped cloves garlic
  • 2 sliced carrot
  • 2 tablespoons plain flour
  • 4 teaspoons curry powder
  • 600ml chicken stock

Instructions

  1. Prep:15min › Cook:30min › Ready in:45min
  2. For the curry sauce: Heat oil in medium non-stick saucepan, add onion and garlic and cook until softened. Stir in carrot
  3. Add flour and curry powder; cook for 1 minute. Gradually stir in stock until combined; add honey, soy sauce and bay leaf
  4. Turn down heat and simmer for 20 minutes or until sauce thickens but is still of pouring consistency. Stir in garam masa
  5. For the chicken: Season both sides of chicken breasts with salt and pepper. Place flour, egg and breadcrumbs in separate
  6. Heat oil in large frying pan over medium-high heat. Place chicken into hot oil and cook until golden brown, about 3 or 4
