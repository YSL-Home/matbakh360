Ingredients

  • 125g Custard
  • 50g Caster Sugar
  • 5 tbs Cocoa
  • 1 beaten Egg
  • 200g shredded Digestive Biscuits
  • 100g Desiccated Coconut
  • 50g Almonds
  • 100g Butter
  • 4 tbs Double Cream
  • 3 tbs Custard Powder
  • 250g Icing Sugar
  • 150g Dark Chocolate

Instructions

  1. Start by making the biscuit base. In a bowl, over a pan of simmering water, melt the butter with the sugar and cocoa pow
  2. For the middle layer, make the custard icing; whisk together the butter, cream and custard powder until light and fluffy
  3. Melt the chocolate and butter together in the microwave, then spread over the chilled bars and put back in the fridge. L
