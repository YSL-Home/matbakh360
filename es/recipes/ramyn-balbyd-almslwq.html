Ingredients

  • 1 Packet Noodles
  • 2 cups Water
  • 1 large Egg
  • 1 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Sauce
  • 1 tbls Spring Onions
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 tbs Pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil: Bring water to a boil, gently add eggs, and cook for exactly 6½ to 7 minutes.
  2. Ice Bath: Immediately transfer the eggs to a bowl of ice water for 3–5 minutes to stop cooking.
  3. Marinate (Optional but recommended): Peel the eggs and marinate in a mix of 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 tsp sugar,
  4. Serve: Slice in half, letting the yolk flow into the broth
